[ 01 ]The moment

The next move isthe one to get right.

Mira Home has identified an opportunity to capture additional traffic and demand in Spring 2027. Partnering with Kaldera Analytics will provide the strategic guidance and ongoing collaboration needed to prepare for launch and capitalize on that opportunity.

Mira Home is ready to scale Google Ads and Local Services Ads. New campaigns cost more per customer while they learn, and the efficiency you’ve built is worth protecting as spend grows.

The work is deciding how much, for how long, and when to pull back.

What to protect
Acquisition cost
What to scale
Google Ads and LSA
When to be ready
Spring 2027

[ 02 ]Our view

Spend is easy to add.CAC is hard to win back.

So the launch goes market by market, and nothing scales until the step before it holds.

  1. Step 1

    Catch every lead

    Every quote form and call answered and booked, nights and weekends too. Missed calls can cost LSA rank.

  2. Step 2

    Trace every dollar

    Every new customer tied to the campaign and market that produced it, so spend moves on evidence.

  3. Step 3

    Set the guardrails

    CAC ceilings, pull-back rules and first-year cancellation checks for each market, agreed before launch.

[ 07 ]Terms

Clear terms,no surprises.

The full language goes in a short services agreement.

  1. 01

    Advice only

    We recommend; Mira Home decides and implements. We don’t make changes in your ad accounts, website, CRM or tracking.

  2. 02

    No guaranteed results

    Performance depends on markets, platforms and execution outside our control.

  3. 03

    Liability

    Our liability is capped at the fees paid. Mira Home defends and indemnifies us against third-party claims tied to its ads, website, lead handling, or how our advice is put into practice.

  4. 04

    Confidentiality

    Your numbers and plans stay private.
